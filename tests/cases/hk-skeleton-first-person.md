SKELETON SUBMISSIONS OF THE PLAINTIFF

1. This is the hearing of the Plaintiff's Summons for discovery. I submit that the Defendant has had every opportunity to comply and has not done so at any point.
2. I have read the Defendant's letters and I think they show no explanation for the delay, which has now lasted many months without any reason being given.

CONCLUSION

3. The Plaintiff invites the Court to make the order sought, with costs to the Plaintiff.
4. In conclusion, the Defendant has no answer and the Court should grant the order in terms of the Summons.
