# Variant `particulars-request`: request for particulars, bill of particulars, or its US substitute

## What it is for

A letter asking the other side to give particulars of its pleading: who, what, when, where, how much, and on what basis, so that the client can plead to it and prepare for trial. The form depends on the court:

- **Hong Kong.** A request for further and better particulars under O.18 r.12. The court may refuse an order for particulars if the applicant did not first ask by letter (O.18 r.12(6)), so this letter is also the first step towards an application, and the judge on that application will read it.
- **New York.** A demand for a bill of particulars under CPLR 3041 and 3042: a captioned paper served on the other side's attorneys, item by item, which must be answered within thirty days, with any objection stated with reasonable particularity.
- **Other US courts (federal, California, Washington, Delaware Superior and Chancery).** There is no request for particulars. Tell the user so, in one or two sentences before the draft: detail comes through discovery (interrogatories, requests for production, depositions), and the pleading-stage remedy is a motion (a motion for a more definite statement under FRCP 12(e), Del. Super. Ct. Civ. R. 12(e), Del. Ch. Ct. R. 12(e) or Wash. CR 12(e), the last somewhat broader; in California, a special demurrer for uncertainty, after the meet and confer the Code of Civil Procedure requires). Then offer one of two letters: a **meet-and-confer letter** before that motion, identifying each vague allegation by paragraph and the detail needed; or a **deficiency letter** on discovery responses already served, as the conferral step before a motion to compel. Use the same discipline as the Hong Kong form: one item per heading, the other side's words quoted, a clear request, a deadline.

## What may be asked for

Particulars tell the other side the case it must meet. They are not evidence, documents or argument. Ask for:

- the facts behind a general allegation: the date, place, persons, and the act or omission;
- for misrepresentation, fraud, breach of trust, wilful default or undue influence (O.18 r.12(1)(a)): who made each representation or did each act, what it was, whether oral or written (and if written, the document), why it is said to be false, and when and how the falsity is said to have come to light; for an oral representation, the precise words used, insofar as they are material;
- for an alleged condition of mind (r.12(1)(b)), the facts relied on; for alleged knowledge or notice (r.12(4)), the facts relied on or the particulars of the notice;
- for damages, each head and how it is calculated, and the facts relied on in mitigation (r.12(1)(c));
- the terms of any agreement relied on: whether written, oral or by conduct, its date and parties, and the clauses.

Do not ask how a fact will be proved, for a list of witnesses, or for copies of documents. A request that fishes for evidence invites an answer that it is improper, and the judge on the application will see it.

A request made before the client's defence is served is ordered only if the particulars are needed to plead or for some other special reason (O.18 r.12(5)). If the client needs the particulars to plead, say so in the purpose line, and ask the other side to agree that time for the defence runs from service of the particulars.

## Structure (Hong Kong)

1. **Header.** `Our Ref.:`, `Your Ref.:`, `Date:`, the mode of delivery ("BY EMAIL ([address]) AND BY POST"), the addressee firm and address, "Attn:", "Dear Sirs,".
2. **"Re:" box.** The case number with its defined term ("the Action"); each party by role and name, with "our client" marked; "and others" for a long list; the subject ("Request for Further and Better Particulars of the [document title]").
3. **Opening.** "We refer to the Action."
4. **Purpose line.** That the request is made under O.18 r.12 (of the Rules of the High Court, or of the Rules of the District Court in that court), to save the time and costs of an application, naming the pleading and its date.
5. **The requests.** In the order of the pleading, under bold headings "Under paragraph [number]" (or "Under paragraphs [number 1] to [number 2]"). Under each: the pleading's own words in quotation marks; one request sentence that opens "Please provide …" or "Please state …"; where needed, a lettered list after "including but not limited to:". Number every request and every sub-item, because the answer must set out each request followed immediately by its answer (O.18 r.12(7)).
6. **Deadline and consequence.** The date by which the particulars are to be served, and that the client will otherwise apply to the Court under O.18 r.12 and ask for the costs of the application.
7. **Protective lines.** The request is made without prejudice to the client's pleading. The client's rights are reserved, including the right to ask for further particulars of the answers.
8. **Sign-off.** "Yours faithfully," then the firm. A copy list ("c.c. Client; Counsel") if the user wants one.

## Differences for the US profiles

- **New York demand.** A captioned paper, not a letter: the caption; "DEMAND FOR A BILL OF PARTICULARS"; "PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that …" demanding a bill under CPLR 3041 and 3042 within thirty days; numbered items ("1. State …"), grouped under "Under paragraph [number] of the Complaint" headings if that helps the answer follow; the date; the signature block of the attorneys for the demanding party; "TO:" the attorneys for the other side. Ask whether the pleading was verified, because a bill responding to a verified pleading may need verification (check CPLR 3044). In a personal-injury action CPLR 3043 lists particulars that may be demanded. Serve it with a short covering letter (variant `covering`).
- **Meet-and-confer letter (federal, Delaware, Washington, California).** "Re:" line with the case caption and number; the rule that requires or invites conferral (FRCP 12(e) for the motion; in California the meet-and-confer section before a demurrer; any local rule or standing order, as `[rule]` until confirmed); headings by paragraph of the pleading, each quoting the allegation and stating the detail needed; a proposed time to confer; a deadline; a reservation of rights.
- **Deficiency letter (discovery).** Headings by request ("Interrogatory No. [number]", "Request for Production No. [number]"); under each, the request and response quoted, the deficiency in one or two sentences, and "Please supplement your response by [date]."; a proposed time to confer; that the client reserves the right to move to compel. A motion to compel in federal court must certify good-faith conferral (FRCP 37(a)(1)); check the local rules and the judge's standing orders for any further step.
- **Sign-off.** "Very truly yours," or "Sincerely," then `[counsel-name]` and the firm.

## Skeleton (Hong Kong)

The ellipses mark the drafter's own words. Bracketed labels are fields from `references/fields.md`.

```
Our Ref.: [number 1]
Your Ref.: [number 2]
Date: [date 1]

BY EMAIL ([address 1]) AND BY POST

[solicitors-firm 1]
[address 2]

Attn: [name 1]

Dear Sirs,

Re: [case number] ("the Action")
[Plaintiff]: [name 2]
[Defendant]: [name 3] ("our client")
Request for Further and Better Particulars of the [document title] dated [date 2]

We refer to the Action.

Pursuant to Order 18 rule 12 of the Rules of the High Court, and to save the time and costs of an application to the Court, we ask your client to provide the following further and better particulars of its [document title] dated [date 2]:

Under paragraph [number 3]

1. Of "…":
   Please provide full particulars of the representation alleged, including but not limited to:
   (a) who made it, and to whom;
   (b) when and where it was made;
   (c) whether it was oral or in writing and, if in writing, the document in which it is said to be contained;
   (d) if oral, the precise words used, insofar as they are material; and
   (e) in what respect it is alleged to have been false.

Under paragraphs [number 4] to [number 5]

2. Of "…":
   Please state with full particularity each act said to amount to the alleged breach, with its date.

3. Please identify each sum allegedly paid, with its date, payer and payee.

Please provide the above particulars on or before [date 3]. Failing which, our client will apply to the Court for an order under Order 18 rule 12 without further notice, and will ask that your client pay the costs of the application.

This request is made without prejudice to our client's [document title].

Our client's rights are expressly reserved, including its right to request further particulars of any answer your client gives.

Yours faithfully,

[solicitors-firm 2]

c.c. Client
```

## Skeleton (US deficiency letter)

```
[date 1]

VIA EMAIL ([address 1])

[counsel-name 1]
[solicitors-firm 1]
[address 2]

Re: [Plaintiff] v. [Defendant], [case number] ([court])

Dear Counsel:

We write on behalf of [Defendant] under Fed. R. Civ. P. 37(a)(1) and [rule] regarding [Plaintiff]'s responses to [Defendant]'s First Set of Interrogatories, served on [date 2].

Interrogatory No. [number 1]

The interrogatory asks: "…". The response states: "…". The response does not … . Please supplement your response by [date 3].

Interrogatory No. [number 2]

…

We are available to meet and confer by telephone or video conference on [date 4] at [time]. If the deficiencies are not cured by [date 3], [Defendant] intends to move to compel. [Defendant] reserves all rights.

Very truly yours,

[counsel-name 2]
[solicitors-firm 2]
```

`[counsel-name 1]` is the attorney written to, `[counsel-name 2]` the attorney signing.

## Checklist

What the checker tests:

- a sign-off at the end and a "Re:" line near the start (soft);
- Hong Kong: "Under paragraph" headings, "Please provide" or "Please state", a reference to O.18 r.12, and the without-prejudice-to-our-pleading line; New York: items grouped by paragraph and a reference to CPLR 3041 or 3042 (both soft), and a sign-off or the attorneys' signature block;
- a reservation of rights, and a deadline (soft);
- other US courts: a reference to meeting and conferring or to the deficiency (soft);
- no "It is submitted" (soft); every placeholder listed in "FIELDS TO COMPLETE".

What the drafter checks by eye:

- every request quotes the pleading's own words and follows the pleading's order;
- each request asks for particulars of the case, not evidence or documents;
- every contested fact is "alleged" or "allegedly";
- the numbering lets the answer follow item by item;
- the deadline and the consequence are what the client has authorised;
- for a request before the defence, the purpose line says why the particulars are needed to plead.
