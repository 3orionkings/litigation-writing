# Variant `covering`: covering letter serving a pleading, amendment or bundle

## What it is for

A short letter that sends a document to the other side and says, for the record, what it is, that it is served, under which rule or order, and what follows from it: the time for the other side's next step, the position on any extension, and anything the other side must do (acknowledge receipt, agree an index). It may be exhibited on a later application about time, so the dates in it must be right.

This variant is built from general practice and the rules, not from a precedent. Keep it to what the document and the timetable need. Do not use a covering letter to argue the document it encloses.

## Structure (Hong Kong)

1. **Header.** References, date, mode of delivery, addressee, "Attn:", salutation. Mark it "URGENT" in the delivery line if a deadline is close ("URGENT BY EMAIL ([address]) AND BY HAND"). The method of service must be one the rules allow for the document and the recipient (O.65, `[rule]`); service by email only where the rules or the other side's agreement permit it.
2. **"Re:" line.** The case number with "the Action", the parties, and the subject ("Service of the Amended Statement of Claim").
3. **What is served.** "We enclose, by way of service, …": the title of the document, its date, and a defined short name. For an amended pleading, the basis of the amendment: without leave under O.20 r.3 (before pleadings close), by consent, or under the order of `[judge]` dated `[date]`; and the colour of the amendments if the user gives it.
4. **What follows.** The time for the other side's next step and its source (the rule, or the order with its date and the judge or master who made it), with the date it ends. State the client's position on any extension only if instructed.
5. **What is asked.** Acknowledgement of receipt, or of service on the client's behalf; for a bundle, agreement of the index by a date.
6. **Reservation of rights.**
7. **Sign-off.**

**For a bundle**, list what is enclosed by bundle letter and volume, with the page range, and the index; say which practice direction the bundles are prepared under (`[rule]`); and ask the other side to agree the index, or say what it wants added, by `[date]`. Use the same lettering the submissions will use in their bundle legend.

## Differences for the US profiles

- **Service is by rule, often electronic.** Papers after the complaint are served under FRCP 5 or the state equivalent (CPLR 2103; Cal. Code Civ. Proc. §§ 1010 to 1013; Wash. CR 5; Del. Super. Ct. Civ. R. 5; Del. Ch. Ct. R. 5), and filing through the court's electronic system usually serves registered counsel. The covering letter is then a courtesy, and the document itself carries the certificate or proof of service in the court's form.
- **Amended pleadings.** Say whether the amendment is as of course, by written consent, or by leave (FRCP 15(a) and the state equivalents), and enclose the comparison the profile requires (for example the document showing changes required in the Delaware Court of Chancery, or the changes shown under CPLR 3025(b)). The time to respond to the amended pleading comes from the rule or the order: `[period]` under `[rule]` until the user confirms it.
- **Opening and sign-off.** "Enclosed please find …" or "Please find enclosed …"; "Very truly yours," or "Sincerely,"; "Enclosure" or "Enclosures"; "cc:".

## Skeleton (Hong Kong, amended pleading)

The ellipses mark the drafter's own words. Bracketed labels are fields from `references/fields.md`.

```
Our Ref.: [number 1]
Your Ref.: [number 2]
Date: [date 1]

URGENT
BY EMAIL ([address 1]) AND BY HAND

[solicitors-firm 1]
[address 2]

Attn: [name 1]

Dear Sirs,

Re: [case number] ("the Action")
[Plaintiff]: [name 2] ("our client")
[Defendant]: [name 3]
Service of the Amended [document title]

We act for our client in the Action.

We enclose our client's Amended [document title] dated [date 1] ("the Amended Pleading"), by way of service. It is amended without leave under Order 20 rule 3 of the Rules of the High Court.

Under the Order of [judge] dated [date 2], your client's [document title] is to be served on or before [date 3]. Our client does not agree to any further extension of time.

Please acknowledge receipt.

All our client's rights are hereby expressly reserved.

Yours faithfully,

[solicitors-firm 2]

Encl.
c.c. Client
```

Write the basis of amendment the user confirms. If leave was given, replace "without leave under Order 20 rule 3 …" with "pursuant to the Order of [judge] dated [date]". In the District Court, the rule is in the Rules of the District Court. Leave out the extension sentence unless the client has given that instruction.

## Checklist

What the checker tests:

- a statement that a document is enclosed or served;
- a sign-off at the end and a "Re:" line near the start (soft);
- a request to acknowledge receipt, or a certificate or proof of service (soft);
- a reservation of rights (soft).

What the drafter checks by eye:

- the title and date of the enclosed document match the document exactly, including "Amended" or "Re-Amended";
- the basis of any amendment is the one the user confirmed;
- the deadline, the order it comes from and the date it ends are right;
- the extension position is the client's instruction, or is left out;
- the method of service is one the rules allow.
