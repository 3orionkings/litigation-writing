# Letter formulae

These are the fixed forms of litigation correspondence. Use them word for word and keep them through the register pass. The tag says where each belongs: **HK** (Hong Kong solicitors' letters), **US** (US attorneys' letters and papers), **both**. Ellipses mark the drafter's own words; bracketed labels are fields. Where both firms appear unfilled, the addressee is `[solicitors-firm 1]` and the firm signing `[solicitors-firm 2]`.

## Header

| Formula | Where |
|---|---|
| "Our Ref.: [number]" / "Your Ref.: [number]" / "Date: [date]" | HK |
| "BY EMAIL ([address]) AND BY POST" / "BY FAX ([number]) AND BY POST" / "BY HAND" / "URGENT BY EMAIL ([address]) AND BY HAND" | HK |
| "VIA EMAIL ([address])" / "VIA EMAIL AND CERTIFIED MAIL, RETURN RECEIPT REQUESTED" / "VIA OVERNIGHT COURIER" | US |
| "Messrs. [solicitors-firm 1]" then the address lines | HK |
| "Attn: [name]" | both |
| "Dear Sirs," (to a firm) / "Dear [name]," (to a named person) | HK |
| "Dear Counsel:" / "Dear [name]:" | US |

## The "Re:" line

| Formula | Where |
|---|---|
| "Re: [case number] ("the Action")" | HK |
| "[Plaintiff]: [name]" / "[Defendant]: [name] ("our client")" / "and others" | HK |
| "Re: [name] ("our client") and [name] ("your client")" (before proceedings) | both |
| "Re: [Plaintiff] v. [Defendant], [case number] ([court])" | US |
| "Request for Further and Better Particulars of the [document title] dated [date]" | HK |

| Formula | Where |
|---|---|
| "WITHOUT PREJUDICE" | HK |
| "WITHOUT PREJUDICE SAVE AS TO COSTS" | HK |
| "OPEN LETTER" | both |
| "SUBJECT TO CONTRACT" (terms not binding until a formal agreement) | HK |
| "CONFIDENTIAL SETTLEMENT COMMUNICATION" / "SUBJECT TO FED. R. EVID. 408" (or the state rule) | US |
| "FOR SETTLEMENT PURPOSES ONLY" | US |

## Opening

| Formula | Where |
|---|---|
| "We refer to the Action." | HK |
| "We refer to your letter dated [date]." | both |
| "We act for [name] ("our client")." | HK |
| "This firm represents [name] in connection with …" / "in the above-referenced matter." | US |
| "We are instructed to …" | both |
| "We write regarding …" / "We write on behalf of [Party] regarding …" | US |

## Particulars

| Formula | Where |
|---|---|
| "Pursuant to Order 18 rule 12 of the Rules of the High Court, and to save the time and costs of an application to the Court, we ask your client to provide the following further and better particulars of …" | HK |
| "Under paragraph [number]" / "Under paragraphs [number] to [number]" (bold run-in heading) | HK, NY |
| "Of "…":" (the pleading's words quoted) | HK |
| "Please provide full particulars of …, including but not limited to:" | HK |
| "Please state with full particularity …" | HK |
| "Please identify …" / "Please specify …" | both |
| "the precise words used, insofar as they are material" | HK |
| "the alleged …" / "allegedly …" (before each contested fact) | both |
| "This request is made without prejudice to our client's [document title]." | HK |
| "Our client's rights are expressly reserved, including its right to request further particulars of any answer your client gives." | HK |
| "PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that [Defendant] demands, pursuant to CPLR 3041 and 3042, that [Plaintiff] serve a bill of particulars of the [document title] within thirty (30) days of service of this demand, setting forth the following:" | NY |

## US conferral

| Formula | Where |
|---|---|
| "We write pursuant to Fed. R. Civ. P. 37(a)(1) and [rule] to meet and confer regarding …" | US |
| "Please supplement your response by [date]." | US |
| "We are available to meet and confer by telephone or video conference on [date] at [time]." | US |
| "[Party] reserves all rights, including the right to move to compel." | US |

## Demand

| Formula | Where |
|---|---|
| "Our client demands that your client pay the sum of [sum] … on or before [date]." | both |
| "together with interest at [rate] from [date] under [statutory provision]" | both |
| "Failing which, our client will commence proceedings against your client without further notice." | HK |
| "… and will seek the costs of those proceedings." | HK |
| "Please confirm whether you have instructions to accept service of proceedings on behalf of your client." | HK |
| "Your client should preserve all documents, including electronic records, relating to …" | both |

## Proposals and offers

| Formula | Where |
|---|---|
| "We are instructed to put forward the following proposal to settle the Action." | HK |
| "in full and final settlement of all claims in the Action" | HK |
| "each party to bear its own costs" / "costs to be taxed if not agreed" | HK |
| "This proposal is made without any admission of liability." | both |
| "This proposal is open for acceptance in writing until [time] on [date], after which it lapses without further notice." | both |
| "Our client reserves the right to bring this letter to the attention of the Court on the question of costs." | HK |
| "SANCTIONED OFFER PURSUANT TO ORDER 22 OF THE RULES OF THE HIGH COURT (CAP. 4A)" / "… OF THE RULES OF THE DISTRICT COURT (CAP. 336H)" | HK |
| "The offer is open for acceptance for [period] from the date of service of this letter, that is until [date]." | HK |
| "Pursuant to Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 68, [Defendant] offers to allow judgment to be taken against it in this action in favor of [Plaintiff] in the amount of [sum] …" | US (federal) |
| "This offer is made for the purposes of Rule 68 only and is not an admission of liability." | US (federal) |
| "If [Plaintiff] does not accept this offer in writing within fourteen (14) days after service, it is deemed withdrawn." | US (federal) |
