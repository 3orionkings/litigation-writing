# Draft or proposed order

A draft order is the relief written as the court would make it. Drafting it early fixes the wording that the summons, the evidence and the submissions all ask for, and saves the court time.

## Hong Kong: draft order

```
[case number]

IN THE HIGH COURT OF THE
HONG KONG SPECIAL ADMINISTRATIVE REGION
COURT OF FIRST INSTANCE

BETWEEN
                [name]                          [Plaintiff]
                and
                [name]                          [Defendant]

BEFORE [judge] IN CHAMBERS

DRAFT ORDER

UPON the application of the [Plaintiff] by summons dated [date 1]

AND UPON HEARING counsel for the [Plaintiff] and counsel for the [Defendant]

AND UPON READING the [number] affirmation of [deponent] filed on [date 2] and the exhibits referred to in it

IT IS ORDERED that:

1. [the first order];
2. Unless the [Defendant] files and serves [document title] by 4:00 pm on [date 3], [the consequence];
3. [directions, each with a date];
4. The costs of and occasioned by this application be paid by the [Defendant] to the [Plaintiff], to be taxed if not agreed, with a certificate for counsel.

Dated the [date 4].
                                        Registrar
```

- **Unless orders.** State the act, the deadline as a date and time, and the consequence in full ("judgment be entered against the [Defendant] for [sum] with interest at [rate] from [date] and costs"). Compute the deadline from the order date only if the user gives the period; otherwise `[date]`.
- **Directions.** One numbered paragraph each, each with its date, in the order the steps happen.
- **Costs.** Who pays whom, the basis (party and party or indemnity), assessment (taxed if not agreed, or summarily assessed at `[sum]`), when (forthwith, or in the cause), and any certificate for counsel.

## United States: proposed order

```
[court]

[name], [Plaintiff], v. [name], [Defendant].        Case No. [case number]

[PROPOSED] ORDER [document title]

The motion of [Defendant] for [the relief] came on for hearing on [date 1]. Having considered the papers filed in support of and in opposition to the motion, [and the arguments of counsel,] and good cause appearing, IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that:

1. The motion is GRANTED [in part].
2. [the order, in full].

IT IS SO ORDERED.

Dated: ____________                       ______________________
                                          [judge]
```

- California: a proposed order submitted with the papers is lodged separately, and after a tentative ruling the prevailing party prepares an order repeating it (read the profile). Washington (King County): every motion includes a proposed order, and an editable version for working copies. New York Commercial Division: no proposed order on a dispositive motion.
- In summary judgment orders in Washington, the order lists the evidence the court considered (CR 56(h)).

## Checklist

The checker tests: numbered orders, no argument, no first person, placeholders listed. Check by eye: every order is one the court has power to make; dates, sums and parties match the evidence; the costs order is complete; the wording matches the summons or notice of motion.
