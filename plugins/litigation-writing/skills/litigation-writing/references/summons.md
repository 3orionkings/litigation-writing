# Summons, notice of motion, motion

The application document says who applies, for what orders, under which rule, and when and where it will be heard. It contains no argument and no evidence.

## Hong Kong: summons

**Inter partes summons.** Heading (court, action number, parties as in the writ), "SUMMONS", the rules relied on in the margin or under the title, the call to attend, the orders sought as numbered paragraphs, costs, date and the Registrar's signature line, who took it out, the time estimate, and the addressees.

```
[case number]

IN THE HIGH COURT OF THE
HONG KONG SPECIAL ADMINISTRATIVE REGION
COURT OF FIRST INSTANCE
[court]

BETWEEN
                [name]                          [Plaintiff]
                and
                [name]                          [Defendant]

SUMMONS

[rule]

LET ALL PARTIES CONCERNED ATTEND before [judge] in Chambers [address] on [date 1] at [time] on the hearing of an application on the part of the [Plaintiff] for an order that:

1. [the first order sought, in court language];
2. [the second order sought];
3. The costs of and occasioned by this application be paid by the [Defendant] to the [Plaintiff], to be taxed if not agreed.

Dated the [date 2].

                                        Registrar

THIS SUMMONS was taken out by [solicitors-firm], solicitors for the [Plaintiff].

Estimated length of hearing: [period]

To: [solicitors-firm], solicitors for the [Defendant]
```

- Use the court's current form and address lines; the user fills the hearing slot after issue (`[judge]`, `[date]`, `[time]` are later fields).
- An **ex parte** application is made without notice: say "ex parte" in the title and omit the addressees unless the court requires service.
- For an application in the Family Court or District Court, use that court's heading and action type; the profile gives the names.
- The orders sought here must match, word for word, the purpose paragraph of the supporting evidence and the draft order.

## United States: notice of motion or motion

Every court frames this differently; read the profile. In outline:

- **Federal.** A motion states with particularity the grounds and the relief sought (FRCP 7(b)). Many districts want a notice of motion (S.D.N.Y. Local Civil Rule 7.1 lists the notice of motion, the memorandum of law and the supporting declarations); others combine notice and memorandum in one document (N.D. Cal. Civil L.R. 7-2). Follow the district's local rules and the judge's individual practices.
- **New York.** Notice of motion (or order to show cause) stating the return date, the part, and the relief; in the Commercial Division, the exact relief sought appears in the notice and again at the end of the memorandum (Rule 16(a)).
- **California.** The notice of motion states in its opening paragraph the nature of the order sought and the grounds (CRC 3.1110(a)); the first page shows the hearing date, time, place and judge; the papers are the notice, the motion and a memorandum (CRC 3.1112). A demurrer or motion to strike follows a meet-and-confer and its declaration (CCP 430.41, 435.5).
- **Washington (King County).** Note the motion for hearing under the local rules (Notice of Court Date), and file one brief in the set format: relief requested, statement of facts, statement of issues, evidence relied on, authority, with a proposed order (KC LCR 7(b)(4) and (5)).
- **Delaware.** Follow the court's rules and standing orders on motions; Chancery practice expects a proposed order.

Skeleton for a notice of motion:

```
[court]

[name],                                   Case No. [case number]
            [Plaintiff],
      v.                                  NOTICE OF MOTION AND MOTION
[name],                                   [document title]
            [Defendant].                  Date: [date 1]  Time: [time]  [court]  Judge: [judge]

PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that on [date 1] at [time], or as soon thereafter as the matter may be heard, [Defendant] will and hereby does move the Court for an order [the relief sought, in full].

The motion is made under [rule] on the grounds that [the grounds, in one or two sentences, without argument].

The motion is based on this notice, the memorandum [document title], the declaration of [deponent] and its exhibits, the pleadings and records on file, and any argument at the hearing.

Dated: [date 2]                           [counsel-name] ([bar number])
                                          [solicitors-firm]
                                          Attorneys for [Defendant]
```

## Checklist

The checker tests: no argument ("It is submitted"), no first person, the orders numbered (Hong Kong), a costs order (Hong Kong, soft), placeholders listed. Check by eye: the court's current form and heading; the rule; the hearing details left for the court to fill; the relief identical to the evidence and the draft order; any meet-and-confer or pre-motion step done.
