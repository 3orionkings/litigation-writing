# Procedural formulae

Set forms of the courts, to be used word for word. Square brackets are placeholders.

## Hong Kong

**Summons**
- "LET ALL PARTIES CONCERNED ATTEND before [judge] in Chambers [address] on [date] at [time] on the hearing of an application on the part of the [Plaintiff] for an order that:"
- "AND TAKE NOTICE THAT …" (used where a rule requires notice to be given in the summons; take the wording from the rule).
- "THIS SUMMONS was taken out by [solicitors-firm], solicitors for the [Plaintiff]."
- "Estimated length of hearing: [period]"
- "Dated the [date]." / "Registrar"

**Order**
- "UPON the application of the [Plaintiff] by summons dated [date]"
- "AND UPON HEARING counsel for the [Plaintiff] and counsel for the [Defendant]"
- "AND UPON READING the [number] affirmation of [deponent] filed on [date]"
- "IT IS ORDERED that:"
- "Unless the [Defendant] [does the act] by 4:00 pm on [date], [the consequence]."
- "Liberty to apply."
- "The costs of and occasioned by this application be paid by the [Defendant] to the [Plaintiff], to be taxed if not agreed, with a certificate for counsel."
- "Costs in the cause." / "Costs reserved." / "No order as to costs."
- "summarily assessed at [sum], to be paid within [period]" / "to be paid forthwith"
- "on an indemnity basis"

## United States

**Notice of motion**
- "PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that on [date] at [time], or as soon thereafter as the matter may be heard, [Party] will and hereby does move the Court for an order …"
- "The motion is made under [rule] on the grounds that …"
- "The motion is based on this notice, the memorandum, the declaration of [deponent] and the exhibits attached to it, the pleadings and records on file, and any argument presented at the hearing."

**Order**
- "[PROPOSED] ORDER GRANTING [document title]"
- "Having considered the papers filed in support of and in opposition to the motion, and good cause appearing, IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that:"
- "IT IS SO ORDERED."
