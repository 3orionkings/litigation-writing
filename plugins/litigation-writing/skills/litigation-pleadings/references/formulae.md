# Formulae

These are the fixed forms of pleading register. Use them word for word, filling only the placeholders, and do not let the register pass rewrite them. Each group says the jurisdiction it belongs to: **HK** (Hong Kong, Court of First Instance, District Court and Family Court), **US** (all US profiles), or a named US profile. Where a party role varies with the pleading, it appears as a placeholder (`[Defendant]`); in a draft, write the role as it appears in the title ("the 2nd Defendant", "the Intervener").

Text in braces `{...}` is a note to the drafter, not part of the formula: replace it with the user's fact. If the user has not given that fact, use the nearest label from `references/fields.md` and say in the "FIELDS TO COMPLETE" table what belongs there.

## 1. Titles (HK)

- `STATEMENT OF CLAIM`
- `POINTS OF CLAIM`, in family proceedings, with the party where needed: `POINTS OF CLAIM OF THE INTERVENER`
- `DEFENCE`, or `DEFENCE OF THE 1ST DEFENDANT` where defendants plead separately
- `POINTS OF DEFENCE`
- `REPLY`
- `REPLY AND DEFENCE TO COUNTERCLAIM`, with the section headings `REPLY` and `DEFENCE TO COUNTERCLAIM`
- `DEFENCE AND COUNTERCLAIM`, with the section headings `DEFENCE` and `COUNTERCLAIM`
- `AMENDED`, `RE-AMENDED`, `RE-RE-AMENDED` before any of these, by round

## 2. The face of a claim (HK)

- Writ-issue statement (O.18 r.15(3)), under the title of a separately served statement of claim: "(Writ of Summons issued on the [date])"
- Post-writ matters (O.18 r.9): "The matters hereinafter pleaded have arisen since the issue of the writ."
- Writ tail (indorsed claim): "THIS WRIT was issued by [solicitors-firm] of [address 1], Solicitors for the said Plaintiff, whose address is [address 2]."

## 3. Parties and capacity (HK)

- "The Plaintiff is and was at all material times a company incorporated in Hong Kong, with its registered office at [address]."
- "The Defendant is and was at all material times a director and shareholder of [name] (the "Company")."
- "At all material times the Defendant acted as the agent of [name]."
- Party conventions: "The Plaintiff and the Defendant are referred to as "P" and "D"."

## 4. The agreement, terms and performance (HK)

- "By a written agreement dated [date] and made between the Plaintiff and the Defendant ("Agreement"), the Defendant agreed to {obligation} for the sum of [sum] ("Price")."
- Oral agreement: "By an oral agreement made on or about [date] at [address] between the Plaintiff, acting by [name], and the Defendant, it was agreed that:-"
- "The Agreement contained, among others, the following express terms:-" then sub-paragraphs, each ending "(see Clause [number])", with "; and" before the last.
- "The Plaintiff will refer to the Agreement at the trial of this action for its full terms, true meaning and effect."
- Implied term: "It was an implied term of the Agreement that {term}. The term is to be implied because:-" {then the facts}
- Statutory implied term: "It was an implied term of the Agreement, by virtue of [statutory provision], that {term}."
- Readiness: "At all material times the Plaintiff was and remains ready, willing and able to perform its obligations under the Agreement."
- Performance: "On [date], pursuant to Clause [number] of the Agreement, the Plaintiff delivered [property description] to the Defendant."

## 5. Breach, demand and loss (HK)

- "In breach of Clause [number] of the Agreement, the Defendant failed and/or refused to {obligation}."
- "PARTICULARS OF BREACH" {lettered sub-paragraphs, one failure each, with the clause and date}
- "By a letter dated [date 1], the Plaintiff's solicitors demanded payment of [sum] by [date 2]." Then, where true: "No reply was received."
- "Despite demand, the Defendant has failed to pay the said sum or any part of it."
- "By reason of the matters aforesaid, the Plaintiff has suffered loss and damage."
- "PARTICULARS OF LOSS AND DAMAGE" {one head per sub-paragraph, with the sum or "to be assessed"}
- "including but not limited to" {only where the list is genuinely open; never to avoid pleading a known head}

## 6. Interest (HK)

Body and prayer must state the same basis, rate, sum and period.

- Contractual: "Further, the Plaintiff claims interest on [sum] at the rate of [rate] per annum pursuant to Clause [number] of the Agreement from [date 1] until payment."
- Statutory: "Further, the Plaintiff claims interest pursuant to [statutory provision] on such sums as may be found due, at such rate and for such period as the Court thinks fit."
- Compound (where the claim is equitable): "Further or alternatively, the Plaintiff claims compound interest under the equitable jurisdiction of the Court."

## 7. Fraud and states of mind (HK; US equivalents in section 15)

- "The Defendant made the First Representation fraudulently, in that he knew it to be false, alternatively made it recklessly, not caring whether it was true or false." {name the representation by its defined term}
- "PARTICULARS OF FRAUD" {numbered primary facts}
- "The Plaintiff will rely on the following facts and matters as showing that the Defendant knew that the First Representation was false when he made it:-"
- Conviction (O.18 r.7A): "On [date], the Defendant was convicted in [court] of {offence}. The Plaintiff intends to rely on the conviction as evidence that {fact}, which is relevant to paragraph [number] above."
- Alternatives: "Further or alternatively,"; "In the alternative, if (which is denied) {fact}, then {fact}."

## 8. Prayer (HK)

Openings:
- "AND THE PLAINTIFF CLAIMS AGAINST THE DEFENDANT FOR:-"
- "AND THE PLAINTIFF CLAIMS AGAINST THE 1ST AND 2ND DEFENDANTS, AND EACH OF THEM, FOR:-"
- "AND THE INTERVENER CLAIMS:-"
- "AND THE DEFENDANT COUNTERCLAIMS AGAINST THE PLAINTIFF FOR:-"

Heads, numbered (1), (2), (3), in this order: the sum or damages; declarations; specific and proprietary relief; accounts and inquiries; interest; then the tail.
- "The sum of [sum]."
- "Damages to be assessed."
- "Alternatively, damages in lieu of or in addition to specific performance."
- "A declaration that the Defendant holds [property description] on trust for the Plaintiff absolutely (or in such share as the Court may determine)."
- "An order that the Defendant do transfer [property description] to the Plaintiff within [number] days of the date of the order."
- "An injunction restraining the Defendant, whether by himself, his servants or agents or otherwise howsoever, from disposing of, charging or otherwise dealing with [property description]."
- "An account of all sums received by the Defendant from [property description], and payment of all sums found due on the taking of the account."
- "Equitable compensation to be assessed."
- "Interest as aforesaid."

Tail:
- "Further and/or other relief."
- "Liberty to apply."
- "Costs." {or "Costs on an indemnity basis." only where an indemnity-costs clause is pleaded as a material term}

Sectioned prayer, where causes are pleaded in the alternative:
- "Under paragraphs [number 1] to [number 2] above:-"
- "Further or alternatively, under paragraphs [number 3] to [number 4] above:-"
- "In any event:-" {for the tail}

## 9. Conventions of a responsive pleading (HK)

- "Unless otherwise stated, the [Defendant] adopts the abbreviations used in the Statement of Claim, for convenience only and without admitting any fact they may imply."
- "Unless the context requires otherwise, a reference to a numbered paragraph is a reference to that paragraph of the Statement of Claim."
- Reply only: "The Plaintiff joins issue with the [Defendant] upon its Defence save in so far as the same consists of admissions."

## 10. Traverse (HK)

Answers:
- "Paragraph [number] is admitted."
- "Paragraph [number] is denied."
- "Paragraph [number] is not admitted."
- "No admission is made as to paragraph [number]."
- "Save that {fact}, paragraph [number] is admitted."
- "Save and except that {fact}, paragraph [number] is denied."
- "Paragraph [number] is denied. The true position is that {fact}."
- "It is denied that {fact}, whether as alleged or at all."
- "It is denied that the [Defendant] received the sum of [sum] or any part thereof, or any other sum."
- "Insofar as paragraph [number] alleges that {fact}, that allegation is denied."
- "The [Defendant] does not know whether {fact} and makes no admission as to it." {a non-admission needs only the want of knowledge}
- "... and the Plaintiff is put to strict proof thereof." {an addition to an answer, never the whole of it}
- "The [Defendant] avers that:-"
- "The [Defendant] repeats paragraphs [number 1] to [number 2] above."
- "Without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing denial,"
- "Even if (which is denied) {fact}, the [Defendant] avers that {fact}."
- "Paragraph [number] contains no allegation of fact against the [Defendant] and no answer to it is required."

Affirmative matter, each in its own paragraph:
- Limitation: "The Plaintiff's claim for {the cause of action} is barred by [statutory provision]. The cause of action, if any, accrued on or about [date 1], and the limitation period expired on [date 2], before the writ was issued on [date 3]."
- Set-off (O.18 r.17): "The [Defendant] is entitled to set off against the Plaintiff's claim the sum of [sum], being {fact}, in diminution or extinction of that claim." {then PARTICULARS OF SET-OFF}
- Change of position: "Further or alternatively, the [Defendant] received the sum of [sum] in good faith and, in reliance on that receipt, changed its position by {fact}, so that it would be inequitable to require it to repay that sum or any part of it."

Closing:
- "In the premises, the Plaintiff is not entitled to the relief claimed or to any relief."
- General traverse (a backstop only): "Save as hereinbefore expressly admitted or not admitted, the [Defendant] denies each and every allegation contained in the Statement of Claim as if the same were set out herein and traversed seriatim."
- In a defence to counterclaim: "Save as hereinbefore expressly admitted or not admitted, the Plaintiff denies each and every allegation contained in the Counterclaim as if the same were set out herein and traversed seriatim."

## 11. Counterclaim (HK)

- "The [Defendant] repeats paragraphs [number 1] to [number 2] of the Defence."
- Prayer opening as in section 8; heads numbered afresh from (1).
- Against an added party: "AND THE [Defendant] COUNTERCLAIMS AGAINST THE PLAINTIFF AND [name] (THE [Defendant] TO THE COUNTERCLAIM) FOR:-" {the title of the action shows the added party}

## 12. Signature, statement of truth and certificate (HK)

Signature:
- "Dated the [date]."
- "[counsel-name]" / "Counsel for the Plaintiff" {one line per counsel, then one role line}
- "[solicitors-firm]" / "Solicitors for the Plaintiff"

Statement of truth (O.41A), the title exactly as it appears on the face of this pleading:
- Individual party: "I, [name], believe that the facts stated in this [document title] are true."
- Party in family proceedings: "I, [name], the Petitioner in this cause, believe that the facts stated in this [document title] are true."
- Company, signed by an officer: "The Plaintiff believes that the facts stated in this [document title] are true. I am duly authorised by the Plaintiff to sign this statement of truth." Then "Signed:", "Name: [name]", "Position: {office held}", "Date: [date]".
- Signed by the party's solicitor: "The Plaintiff believes that the facts stated in this [document title] are true." Then "[solicitors-firm]", "Solicitors for the Plaintiff", "Date: [date]".

Certificate where the verifier does not read English, after the statement of truth:
- "I, [name], of [solicitors-firm], [address], certify that I am competent in the English language and in {the verifier's language}, and that I have {interpreted/translated} the contents of this document and the statement of truth to the person signing the statement of truth, who appeared to understand (a) the document and approved its content as accurate and (b) the statement of truth and the consequences of making a false statement, and signed in my presence." Then signature, "Name: [name]", "Solicitor, Hong Kong SAR", "Date: [date]". {"interpreted" for a document read aloud, "translated" for a written translation; the pronoun, if any, follows the verifier}

Backsheet date slots, one line each, all three always present:
- "Dated the [date 1]"
- "Filed the [date 2]"
- "Served the [date 3]"
- After amendment, add "Re-filed the [date 4]" under "Filed", and keep "Served".

## 13. Amendment legends and the summons (HK)

Legends, at the head of the first page above the title, one per round, earlier rounds kept:
- Without leave, pleading: "Amended and underlined in red the [date], pursuant to RHC, Order 20, Rule 3"
- Without leave, writ: "Amended and underlined in red the [date], pursuant to RHC, Order 20, Rule 1"
- With leave: "Amended pursuant to the Order of [judge] dated the [date]"
- Re-amendment with leave: "Re-Amended and underlined in green the [date 2], pursuant to the Order of [judge] dated the [date 3]"
- By written agreement: "Amended pursuant to RHC, Order 20, Rule 12"
- Fresh document: "Replaced by Amendment made the [date 1], pursuant to RHC, Order 20, Rule 3" {or "pursuant to the Order of [judge] dated the [date 2]"}
- In the District Court, the corresponding rule of the Rules of the District Court: [rule].

Summons for leave, formal parts:
- "SUMMONS"
- "LET ALL PARTIES concerned attend before [judge] in Chambers at [court] on [date] at [time] on the hearing of an application by the Plaintiff for an Order that:-"
- "1. The Plaintiff do have leave to amend the Statement of Claim in the form of the draft Amended Statement of Claim annexed hereto, the amendments being shown in red;"
- "2. The Plaintiff do file and serve the Amended Statement of Claim within [number] days of the date of the Order;"
- "3. The Defendant do have leave to file and serve an Amended Defence, if so advised, within [number] days after service of the Amended Statement of Claim;"
- "4. The Plaintiff do have leave to file and serve an Amended Reply, if so advised, within [number] days after service of the Amended Defence;"
- "5. The costs of and occasioned by the amendment be borne by the Plaintiff." {the default; "costs in the cause" or "reserved" only if the user chooses it}
- "Dated the [date]." / "Registrar"
- "This Summons was taken out by [solicitors-firm], Solicitors for the Plaintiff, of [address]."
- "Estimated length of hearing: [number] minutes."
- "To: [solicitors-firm], Solicitors for the Defendant, [address]."

## 14. Complaint (US)

- Opening: "Plaintiff [name 1] ("Plaintiff"), by its undersigned attorneys, for its Complaint against Defendant [name 2] ("Defendant"), alleges as follows, upon personal knowledge as to itself and its own acts and upon information and belief as to all other matters:"
- Corporate status (required in New York, CPLR 3015(b)): "Plaintiff is a corporation organized under the laws of {state or country}, with its principal place of business in {state or country}."
- Jurisdiction (US federal): "This Court has subject-matter jurisdiction over this action under [statutory provision] because {fact}."
- Venue (US federal): "Venue is proper in this District under [statutory provision] because {fact}."
- Count heading (US federal, NY, WA, DE): "COUNT I" / "(Breach of Contract)" / "(Against Defendant [name 2])"
- Count heading (CA, CRC 2.112): "FIRST CAUSE OF ACTION" / "(Breach of Contract, by Plaintiff [name 1] against Defendant [name 2])"
- Incorporation: "Plaintiff repeats and realleges each and every allegation in paragraphs 1 through [number] as if fully set forth herein."
- Conditions precedent (US federal, FRCP 9(c); not needed in NY, CPLR 3015(a)): "All conditions precedent to Plaintiff's right to recover have occurred, have been performed, or have been excused."
- Information and belief: "Upon information and belief, {fact}. The basis for this belief is {fact}."
- Prayer: "PRAYER FOR RELIEF" / "WHEREFORE, Plaintiff respectfully requests that the Court enter judgment in its favor and against Defendant as follows:" then heads lettered (a), (b), (c), ending "Such other and further relief as the Court deems just and proper."
- Interest head: "Pre-judgment and post-judgment interest at the rate provided by [statutory provision]."
- Costs head: "Plaintiff's costs of this action and, where a contract or statute so provides, its reasonable attorneys' fees."
- Jury demand: "DEMAND FOR JURY TRIAL" / "Pursuant to [rule], Plaintiff demands a trial by jury on all issues so triable."
- Signature: "Dated: [date]" / "Respectfully submitted," / "[solicitors-firm]" / "By: /s/ [counsel-name]" / "[counsel-name] ([bar number])" / "[address]" / "Attorneys for Plaintiff [name 1]"

## 15. Fraud (US)

- "On [date], Defendant, through [name 3], stated to [name 4] by {medium} that {content} (the "First Representation"). The First Representation was false because {fact}. Defendant knew that it was false when made because {fact}."
- Federal and Chancery (Rule 9(b)): knowledge may be alleged generally, but the circumstances (who, what, when, where, how) must be stated with particularity. California: also by what means and, for a company, the speaker's name and authority. Delaware Superior Court: negligence too must be pleaded with particularity.

## 16. Answer (US)

- Opening: "Defendant [name 2] ("Defendant"), by its undersigned attorneys, answers the Complaint of Plaintiff [name 1] ("Plaintiff") as follows:"
- Headings: "The headings of the Complaint are repeated below for convenience only. To the extent they contain allegations, Defendant denies them."
- Admit: "Defendant admits the allegations of paragraph [number]."
- Deny: "Defendant denies the allegations of paragraph [number]."
- Part: "Defendant admits that {fact}. Defendant otherwise denies the allegations of paragraph [number]."
- Want of knowledge (US federal, FRCP 8(b)(5); DE): "Defendant lacks knowledge or information sufficient to form a belief about the truth of the allegations of paragraph [number], and therefore denies them."
- Want of knowledge (NY, CPLR 3018(a)): "Defendant denies knowledge or information sufficient to form a belief as to the allegations of paragraph [number]."
- Want of knowledge (WA, CR 8(b)): "Defendant is without knowledge or information sufficient to form a belief as to the truth of the allegations of paragraph [number], and therefore denies them."
- Legal conclusion: "Paragraph [number] states conclusions of law to which no response is required. To the extent a response is required, Defendant denies the allegations of paragraph [number]."
- Document: "Defendant admits that [document title] dated [date] contains the words quoted in paragraph [number], refers to the document for its contents, and denies any characterization of it inconsistent with its terms."
- Relief: "Defendant denies that Plaintiff is entitled to the relief requested in the Prayer for Relief or to any relief."
- General denial where the profile allows it (US federal, WA; never DE Chancery): "Except as expressly admitted above, Defendant denies each and every allegation of the Complaint, including any allegation in its headings, unnumbered paragraphs and Prayer for Relief."
- California, unverified complaint (CCP 431.30(d)): "Defendant generally denies each and every allegation of the Complaint, and further denies that Plaintiff has been damaged in any sum or at all."
- Delaware Chancery (Rule 7(a)(3)): repeat each allegation verbatim as an indented quotation under its number, then "RESPONSE:" followed by the response. {New York Commercial Division interlineation, Rule 6(d), uses the same layout}
- Defenses: "AFFIRMATIVE DEFENSES" / "Defendant asserts the following defenses without assuming any burden of proof that it would not otherwise bear."
- Each defense: "FIRST AFFIRMATIVE DEFENSE" / "(Statute of Limitations)" / "Plaintiff's claims are barred, in whole or in part, by [statutory provision] because {fact}." {California: add "(To All Causes of Action)" or name the causes it answers}
- Prayer: "WHEREFORE, Defendant requests that the Court dismiss the Complaint with prejudice, enter judgment in favor of Defendant, award Defendant its costs, and grant such other and further relief as the Court deems just and proper."

## 17. Counterclaim, cross-complaint and reply (US)

- "COUNTERCLAIMS" / "Defendant and Counterclaim-Plaintiff [name 2] alleges as follows against Plaintiff and Counterclaim-Defendant [name 1]:"
- California: a separate pleading titled "CROSS-COMPLAINT", "Cross-Complainant [name 2] alleges against Cross-Defendant [name 1] as follows:"
- Response (US federal): "ANSWER TO COUNTERCLAIMS"; (NY, DE Superior): "REPLY TO COUNTERCLAIMS"; (CA): "ANSWER TO CROSS-COMPLAINT". Opening: "Plaintiff and Counterclaim-Defendant [name 1] answers the Counterclaims as follows:" then the answer grammar of section 16.

## 18. Verification and amendment (US)

- New York (CPLR 3020, 3021, 2106): "[name 3] affirms: I am the {office held} of Plaintiff [name 1]. I have read the foregoing [document title] and know its contents. It is true to my own knowledge, except as to the matters stated to be alleged on information and belief, and as to those matters I believe it to be true." Then: "I affirm this [date], under the penalties of perjury under the laws of New York, which may include a fine or imprisonment, that the foregoing is true, except as to matters alleged on information and belief and as to those matters I believe it to be true, and I understand that this document may be filed in an action or proceeding in a court of law."
- California (CCP 446, 2015.5): "I, [name 3], declare: I am the {office held} of Plaintiff [name 1] and am authorized to make this verification on its behalf. I have read the foregoing [document title] and know its contents. The matters stated in it are true of my own knowledge, except as to those matters stated on information and belief, and as to those matters I believe them to be true. I declare under penalty of perjury under the laws of the State of California that the foregoing is true and correct. Executed on [date] at [address]."
- Delaware Chancery (Rule 3(c)): the party, or an authorised person for an entity, verifies under oath or by affirmation that the matters in the pleading are true and correct to the best of their knowledge, information and belief, before a notary or as the profile states.
- Titles: "FIRST AMENDED COMPLAINT"; "[PROPOSED] FIRST AMENDED COMPLAINT" (WA: marked "proposed" and unsigned).
- Comparison statement: "Exhibit B is a comparison of the proposed First Amended Complaint against the Complaint filed on [date], showing additions underlined and deletions struck through."
- California schedule (CRC 3.1324(a)): "The amendment deletes the allegations at page [number 1], paragraph [number 2], lines [number 3] to [number 4] of the Complaint, and adds the allegations at page [number 5], paragraph [number 6], lines [number 7] to [number 8] of the proposed First Amended Complaint."
