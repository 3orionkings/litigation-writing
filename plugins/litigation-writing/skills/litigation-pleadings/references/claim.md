# Claim: statement of claim, points of claim, complaint

## What it is for

The claim pleads every fact the plaintiff must prove to win each cause of action, and the relief each one supports. It is the document the court will read to decide whether a cause of action is disclosed at all, so a missing element is fatal, while evidence, law and argument only make it longer and easier to attack. Everything the plaintiff wants at trial, including interest and every head of relief, must be in it.

## Hong Kong

### Forms

- **Statement of claim served separately** after the writ (O.18 r.1). Its face states the date the writ was issued, under the title (O.18 r.15(3)).
- **Statement of claim indorsed on the writ.** The writ is the prescribed form, with the claim-nature box, the defendant's address for service and the prescribed notices; take these from the user's or the firm's current form rather than reproducing them. The indorsed statement of claim follows, then the writ tail naming the issuing solicitors. The writ itself carries its issue date.
- **Points of claim in family proceedings.** The same engine. The caption is the Family Court's, the BETWEEN block names the Petitioner, the Respondent and any Intervener, the roles are those, and the statement of truth speaks of "this cause". There is no writ, so no writ-issue line.

### Face

The action number in short form at the top right; the court heading, as in `jurisdictions/hk.md`; the action number in long form; the BETWEEN block with each party's name exactly as in the writ and its role; the title between rules; the writ-issue line. A title that differs from the writ by a letter is a defect, so copy it.

### Structure: inducement, body, prayer

Headings are lettered and named after the facts ("A. THE PARTIES", "C. THE AGREEMENT", "D. THE PAYMENTS FOR THE PROPERTY"), never after a doctrine. Paragraphs run continuously; sub-paragraphs are (a), (b), then (i), (ii).

1. **The parties.** Each party's identity and only the description the claim needs: incorporation, registered office and company number for a company; residence for an individual; capacity ("is and was at all material times a director of [name]") where it matters; a regulatory status the claim turns on. Define short names here.
2. **Background.** Short dated paragraphs, in date order, covering only what explains the claim. It is not a narrative of the relationship.
3. **The transaction.** For a contract: the agreement by title, date and parties, its form, and its material terms clause by clause, each ending "(see Clause [number])"; implied terms with the facts they arise from; the reference to the agreement "for its full terms, true meaning and effect". For other causes, the module in `causes-of-action.md`.
4. **Performance.** What the plaintiff did, or that it was ready, willing and able to perform. Itemise performance where specific performance is claimed.
5. **Breach or wrong.** A conclusion naming the clauses or duties, then PARTICULARS, one failure to a sub-paragraph, with its clause and date.
6. **Demand.** Each demand by date and form, and the response. "No reply was received." where that is true.
7. **Loss.** "By reason of the matters aforesaid, the Plaintiff has suffered loss and damage", then PARTICULARS OF LOSS AND DAMAGE, each head with its figure or "to be assessed". Over three folios, a separate schedule (O.18 r.12(2)).
8. **Interest.** The basis (the clause and rate, or `[statutory provision]`), the sum it runs on, and the period from and to. Repeat it exactly in the prayer. Compound interest is claimed expressly, with its basis.
9. **The prayer.** "AND THE PLAINTIFF CLAIMS AGAINST THE DEFENDANT FOR:-", then each relief numbered, in the order of `formulae.md` section 8, ending with the tail. A relief claimed in the writ and left out of the prayer is abandoned. Where causes are pleaded in the alternative, divide the prayer by the paragraphs each part rests on.
10. **Formal parts.** The dated line; counsel (one line each) and "Counsel for the Plaintiff"; the solicitors and "Solicitors for the Plaintiff"; the statement of truth naming "this Statement of Claim" (or the exact title); any interpreter's or translator's certificate; the backsheet with Dated, Filed and Served slots.

### Rules to hold

- Dates, sums and numbers in figures (O.18 r.6(3)).
- No cause of action outside the writ, and none that arose after it. Post-writ matters that go to relief are introduced by the recital in `formulae.md` section 2 (O.18 rr.9, 15(2)).
- No anticipation of a defence, and no plea of conditions precedent (O.18 r.7(3), (4)).
- Alternatives expressly as alternatives, with the primary case identified (O.18 r.12A). A short bridging paragraph says which claims are pleaded in the alternative to which.
- For parallel actions against several defendants on one set of facts, keep the common facts and each defendant's own facts (names, dates, sums, ordinals, registration details) apart, render each claim from its own facts, and check every recurring figure and party reference in each.

## United States: what changes

- **Name and parts.** "Complaint". Caption with the court, all parties, the case number and the designation; jurisdiction and venue (federal; a separate "Jurisdiction" section is required in some districts); the parties; factual allegations; counts; the prayer for relief or WHEREFORE clause; a jury demand where wanted; the signature block with `[bar number]`. California and the Northern District of California put counsel's details at the top left of page one; the profile gives the paper format (line-numbered pleading paper in California; the Washington first-page margin).
- **Standard.** Federal: enough facts to make liability plausible, so plead what happened rather than the elements in the law's words. New York: the material elements of each cause and notice of the transactions (CPLR 3013). California: the facts constituting each cause of action (CCP 425.10). Washington: notice pleading. Delaware: as its profile states. Fraud everywhere with particularity (see `causes-of-action.md` modules 3 and 4).
- **Counts.** Each cause of action is a separately numbered count with its heading (`formulae.md` section 14), opening by repeating and realleging the earlier paragraphs. California headings name the number, the nature, by whom and against whom (CRC 2.112).
- **Allegations on belief.** Facts not within the plaintiff's knowledge are alleged "upon information and belief", with the basis where the profile or Rule 9(b) requires it.
- **Diversity.** A diversity complaint pleads the citizenship of every party, and of every member of an LLC or partnership; some districts require it in terms.
- **Sums.** California states the amount demanded, except in personal injury and wrongful death (CCP 425.10); New York states no figure in personal injury (CPLR 3017(c)); Delaware Superior Court states none for unliquidated damages (Rule 9(g)).
- **Exhibits.** A written instrument attached to the complaint becomes part of it (FRCP 10(c); CPLR 3014). Attach the contract where the user wants it in; Hong Kong practice usually does not.
- **Verification.** Not required in most courts; required for complaints in the Court of Chancery (Rule 3(c)); optional in New York and California, but once used it forces a verified answer. Forms in `formulae.md` section 18.
- **No statement of truth and no backsheet.** The attorney's signature certifies the pleading.

## Skeleton: Hong Kong statement of claim (contract)

Text in braces is a drafting note. Write the parties' roles as they appear in the title.

```
                                                            [case number]
{court heading lines, from the profile}
ACTION NO. [case number]

BETWEEN

[name 1]                                                          Plaintiff

and

[name 2]                                                          Defendant

{rule}
STATEMENT OF CLAIM
{rule}
(Writ of Summons issued on the [date 1])

A. THE PARTIES

1. The Plaintiff is and was at all material times a company incorporated in
   {place}, with company number [company number] and its registered office
   at [address].

2. The Defendant:-
   (a) is an individual residing at [address]; and
   (b) is and was at all material times a director of [name 3] (the "Company").

B. BACKGROUND

3. On [date 2], {fact}.

C. THE AGREEMENT

4. By a written agreement dated [date 3] and made between the Plaintiff and
   the Defendant ("Agreement"), the Defendant agreed to purchase {subject
   matter} from the Plaintiff for the sum of [sum 1] ("Price").

5. The Agreement contained, among others, the following express terms:-
   (a) the Defendant would pay the Price on or before [date 4] ("Completion
       Date") (see Clause [number]);
   (b) {term} (see Clause [number]); and
   (c) any part of the Price unpaid after the Completion Date would carry
       interest at [rate] per annum until payment (see Clause [number]).

6. It was an implied term of the Agreement that {term}. The term is to be
   implied because:-
   (a) {fact known to both parties when the Agreement was made};
   (b) {fact}.

7. The Plaintiff will refer to the Agreement at the trial of this action for
   its full terms, true meaning and effect.

D. PERFORMANCE AND BREACH

8. At all material times the Plaintiff was ready, willing and able to perform
   its obligations under the Agreement. On [date 5], pursuant to Clause
   [number] of the Agreement, the Plaintiff {act of performance}.

9. In breach of Clauses [number 1] and [number 2] of the Agreement, the
   Defendant failed to pay the Price or any part of it on the Completion Date
   or at all.

10. By a letter dated [date 6], the Plaintiff's solicitors demanded payment of
    the Price by [date 7]. No reply was received.

E. LOSS AND DAMAGE

11. By reason of the matters aforesaid, the Plaintiff has suffered loss and
    damage.

                    PARTICULARS OF LOSS AND DAMAGE

    (a) The Price: [sum 1].
    (b) {head of loss}: [sum 2].

12. Further, the Plaintiff claims interest on [sum 1] at the rate of [rate]
    per annum pursuant to Clause [number] of the Agreement from [date 4] until
    payment, alternatively interest pursuant to [statutory provision] at such
    rate and for such period as the Court thinks fit.

AND THE PLAINTIFF CLAIMS AGAINST THE DEFENDANT FOR:-

(1) The sum of [sum 1].
(2) Further or alternatively, damages to be assessed.
(3) Interest on [sum 1] at the rate of [rate] per annum pursuant to Clause
    [number] of the Agreement from [date 4] until payment, alternatively
    interest pursuant to [statutory provision] at such rate and for such
    period as the Court thinks fit.
(4) Further and/or other relief.
(5) Costs.

Dated the [date 8].

                                                    [counsel-name]
                                                    Counsel for the Plaintiff

                                                    [solicitors-firm]
                                                    Solicitors for the Plaintiff

STATEMENT OF TRUTH

The Plaintiff believes that the facts stated in this Statement of Claim are
true. I am duly authorised by the Plaintiff to sign this statement of truth.

Signed:
Name: [name 4]
Position: {office held}
Date: [date 9]

{backsheet, on its own page}
                                                            [case number]
{court heading lines}
BETWEEN [name 1] Plaintiff and [name 2] Defendant
{rule}
STATEMENT OF CLAIM
{rule}
Dated the [date 8]
Filed the [date 10]
Served the [date 11]
[solicitors-firm]
Solicitors for the Plaintiff
[address]
Tel: [number]   Fax: [number]
Ref: [number]
```

For an indorsed claim, drop the writ-issue line, put the claim on the writ form, and end with the writ tail in `formulae.md` section 2 before the statement of truth. For points of claim, use the Family Court caption, the three-party BETWEEN block, "POINTS OF CLAIM OF THE INTERVENER" (or of the party), "AND THE INTERVENER CLAIMS:-", and the family form of the statement of truth.

```
[counsel-name] ([bar number])
[solicitors-firm]
[address]
Attorneys for Plaintiff [name 1]

                     UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
                     {district}

[name 1],                                       Case No. [case number]
               Plaintiff,
                                              COMPLAINT
     v.
                                              JURY TRIAL DEMANDED
[name 2],
               Defendant.

Plaintiff [name 1] ("Plaintiff"), by its undersigned attorneys, for its
Complaint against Defendant [name 2] ("Defendant"), alleges as follows, upon
personal knowledge as to itself and its own acts and upon information and
belief as to all other matters:

                          NATURE OF THE ACTION

1. {two or three sentences of fact: who did what, and what is claimed}

                              THE PARTIES

2. Plaintiff is a corporation organized under the laws of {state or country},
   with its principal place of business in {state or country}.

3. Defendant is {identity, residence or incorporation, and citizenship}.

                         JURISDICTION AND VENUE

4. This Court has subject-matter jurisdiction over this action under
   [statutory provision] because {the federal question, or complete diversity
   with each party's citizenship and the amount in controversy}.

5. This Court has personal jurisdiction over Defendant because {fact}.

6. Venue is proper in this District under [statutory provision] because
   {fact}.

                          FACTUAL ALLEGATIONS

7. On [date 1], Plaintiff and Defendant entered into a written agreement
   titled [document title] (the "Agreement"). A true copy of the Agreement is
   attached as Exhibit A.

8. Section [number] of the Agreement required Defendant to pay Plaintiff
   [sum 1] on or before [date 2].

9. On [date 3], Plaintiff {act of performance}.

10. Defendant did not pay [sum 1] on [date 2] or at any time since.

11. On [date 4], Plaintiff demanded payment in writing. Defendant did not
    respond.

                                COUNT I
                          (Breach of Contract)
                         (Against Defendant)

12. Plaintiff repeats and realleges each and every allegation in paragraphs
    1 through 11 as if fully set forth herein.

13. The Agreement is a valid and enforceable contract between Plaintiff and
    Defendant.

14. Plaintiff performed its obligations under the Agreement. All conditions
    precedent to Plaintiff's right to recover have occurred, have been
    performed, or have been excused.

15. Defendant breached Section [number] of the Agreement by failing to pay
    [sum 1] when due.

16. As a direct and proximate result of Defendant's breach, Plaintiff has
    been damaged in an amount to be determined at trial, but not less than
    [sum 1].

                           PRAYER FOR RELIEF

WHEREFORE, Plaintiff respectfully requests that the Court enter judgment in
its favor and against Defendant as follows:

(a) Damages in an amount to be determined at trial, but not less than
    [sum 1];
(b) Pre-judgment and post-judgment interest at the rate provided by
    [statutory provision];
(c) Plaintiff's costs of this action and, where a contract or statute so
    provides, its reasonable attorneys' fees; and
(d) Such other and further relief as the Court deems just and proper.

                        DEMAND FOR JURY TRIAL

Pursuant to [rule], Plaintiff demands a trial by jury on all issues so
triable.

Dated: [date 5]                         Respectfully submitted,

                                        [solicitors-firm]

                                        By: /s/ [counsel-name]
                                        [counsel-name] ([bar number])
                                        [address]
                                        Attorneys for Plaintiff [name 1]
```

California: counsel block at the top left from line 1, "FIRST CAUSE OF ACTION (Breach of Contract, by Plaintiff [name 1] against Defendant [name 2])", the amount demanded stated, pleading paper. Delaware Superior Court: no figure for unliquidated damages. Court of Chancery: add the verification. New York: "WHEREFORE" and, where wanted, a verification.

## Formulae

`formulae.md` sections 2 to 8 (Hong Kong) and 14, 15 and 18 (US). Cause-of-action bodies: `causes-of-action.md`.

## Checklist

The checker tests:
- numbered paragraphs.
- a prayer: "AND THE ... CLAIMS" in Hong Kong; PRAYER FOR RELIEF, WHEREFORE or a demand for relief in the US.
- Hong Kong: the writ-issue statement (or the writ itself, or points of claim), a statement of truth, and a line beginning "Served" on the backsheet.
- no "knew or ought to have known" anywhere (hard), and "knew or should have known" flagged for a second look.
- no procedural threats; no intensified denials; no English CPR vocabulary in Hong Kong.
- US: a jurisdiction section in a federal complaint, a bar number in the signature block, and a verification in the Court of Chancery (soft).
- the shared checks: placeholders and the FIELDS table, citations, dashes and tells.

Check by eye:
- every party's name and role identical to the writ or caption.
- the agreement identified by title, date and parties, and its form stated.
- every term relied on, with its clause; every implied term, with its facts.
- every element of every cause of action pleaded as fact, with the particulars its module requires.
- the sum, rate and period of interest identical in body and prayer; every recurring sum identical.
- every relief in the prayer supported by pleaded facts, and every relief in the writ repeated.
- dates and sums in figures; defined terms used consistently; cross-references live.
- nothing pleaded that is law, evidence, argument or a threat.
