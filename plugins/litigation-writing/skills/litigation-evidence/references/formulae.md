# Evidence formulae

These are the fixed forms of written evidence. Use them word for word for the jurisdiction shown, and do not paraphrase them in the register pass. Square brackets are placeholders from `references/fields.md`; text in braces is a drafting note to be replaced or deleted. Where a form comes from a statute or rule, the profile in `references/jurisdictions/<code>.md` has the current text: check it before filing.

Tags: **HK** Hong Kong (all courts); **HK Family** Family Court practice; **Fed** US district courts; **NY** New York; **CA** California; **WA** Washington; **DE** Delaware Superior Court and Court of Chancery; **US** every US profile.

## 1. Opening words

- **HK affirmation.** I, [deponent], of [address], do hereby solemnly, sincerely and truly affirm and say as follows:
- **HK affirmation, shorter form.** I, [deponent], of [address], solemnly and sincerely affirm and say as follows:
- **HK affidavit (sworn).** I, [deponent], of [address], make oath and say as follows:
- **HK non-party.** I, [deponent], {occupation}, of [address], do hereby solemnly, sincerely and truly affirm and say as follows:
- **HK witness statement.** I, [witness], of [address], do make this statement and say as follows:
- **Fed, CA, WA declaration.** I, [deponent], declare as follows:
- **NY affirmation.** I, [deponent], affirm as follows: {the CPLR 2106 words close the document}
- **DE affidavit.** [deponent], being duly sworn, deposes and says as follows: {check the form the court expects}

## 2. Capacity and authority

- **HK party.** I am the [Plaintiff] in this action. / I am the [Petitioner] herein.
- **HK party in person.** I am the [Defendant] in this action, and I act in person.
- **HK company.** I am a director of the [Plaintiff]. I am duly authorised by the [Plaintiff] to make this affirmation on its behalf.
- **HK solicitor.** I am a solicitor of the High Court of Hong Kong and {a partner / an associate} of Messrs. [solicitors-firm], the solicitors for the [Plaintiff]. I have the conduct of this action on behalf of the [Plaintiff], and I am authorised by the [Plaintiff] to make this affirmation on its behalf.
- **HK non-party.** I am not a party to these proceedings. {Relationship to the parties, in one sentence.}
- **HK witness statement.** I am the [Plaintiff] in this action. / I am a director of the [Plaintiff], and I am authorised by the [Plaintiff] to make this witness statement on its behalf.
- **US.** I am {title} of [Defendant]. I am over 18 years of age. I have personal knowledge of the facts stated in this declaration and, if called as a witness, I could and would testify competently to them.
- **US attorney.** I am an attorney admitted to practice before this Court and {a partner} of [solicitors-firm], counsel for [Plaintiff] in this action.

## 3. Knowledge

- **HK.** The facts and matters deposed to herein are, unless otherwise stated, within my own knowledge and are true to the best of my knowledge, information and belief.
- **HK, with sources.** The facts and matters deposed to herein are, unless otherwise stated, within my own knowledge and are true. Where they are not within my own knowledge, I state their source, and they are true to the best of my knowledge, information and belief.
- **HK, company records.** Where I refer to events before {the date the deponent became involved}, I do so from the records of the [Plaintiff], kept in the ordinary course of its business, to which I have access.
- **HK non-party.** Save where otherwise stated, the facts deposed to herein are within my own knowledge. Where they are not, I identify the source of my information, and I believe it to be true.
- **US.** The personal-knowledge and competence sentence in section 2. FRCP 56(c)(4) and its state equivalents require personal knowledge, admissible facts and a competent witness.

## 4. Reading conventions and earlier evidence

- **HK.** Unless otherwise stated, I adopt the abbreviations used in the [document title] dated [date].
- **HK.** If and so far as necessary, I crave leave to refer to the [document title] dated [date].
- **HK.** I refer to my [number] Affirmation made on [date] (my "[number] Affirmation"). I do not repeat its contents save where necessary.
- **HK.** In this affirmation, "[name]'s Affirmation" means the [number] Affirmation of [name] made on [date].

## 5. Purpose

- **HK support.** I make this affirmation in support of the [Plaintiff]'s Summons dated [date] (the "Summons") for the following orders:
- **HK own summons.** I make this affirmation in support of my Summons dated [date] for the following orders:
- **HK opposition.** I make this affirmation in opposition to the [Defendant]'s Summons dated [date] (the "Summons").
- **HK reply.** I make this affirmation in reply to [name]'s Affirmation and in further support of the Summons.
- **HK ex parte.** I make this affirmation in support of the [Judgment Creditor]'s ex parte application under [rule] for an order that {the order sought}.
- **HK order tail.** … or such further or other order as this Honourable Court thinks fit.
- **US.** I submit this declaration in support of [Plaintiff]'s [document title]. / … in opposition to … / … in further support of …

## 6. Belief and its sources

Law (**HK** only; never in a witness statement or a US declaration):

- I am advised and verily believe that
- I am advised by the [Plaintiff]'s solicitors, and verily believe, that
- I am further advised and verily believe that
- {Hand-off, in the deponent's own short sentence:} That is a matter of law, which I leave to counsel.

Information from a person (**HK**):

- I am informed by [name], {role}, and verily believe that
- [name] told me on [date] that {what was said}. I believe what {he} told me to be true.

What a document shows (**HK**):

- From the [document title], I verily believe that
- To the best of my knowledge, information and belief,

Inference from facts already set out (**HK**):

- I verily believe that
- I believe that

Grounds of belief (**HK**, and the substance in **US**):

- My belief is based on {the sources}.
- The grounds of my belief are {the matters set out above}.
- This forms part of the basis of my belief.

What is not known (all):

- I do not know {the gap}.
- {The matter} is not within my knowledge.

Witness statement (**HK**):

- [name] told me, and I believed, that
- It is my understanding that
- I understand from the [document title] that
- To the best of my knowledge and belief,

US:

- On information and belief, based on {the source}, {the fact}. {N.D. Cal. Civil L.R. 7-5(b) requires the basis to be stated; state it everywhere.}

## 7. Exhibits

In the body (**HK**):

- There is now produced and shown to me marked "[exhibit]" a copy of the [document title] dated [date].
- There is now produced and shown to me a copy of the [document title] dated [date] marked "[exhibit]".
- There are now produced and shown to me marked "[exhibit 1]" to "[exhibit 3]" copies of: (a) {document}; (b) {document}; and (c) {document}.
- There is now produced and shown to me a paginated bundle of copy documents marked "[exhibit]". References to page numbers in this affirmation are to pages of that bundle.
- A copy of the [document title] is at pages [number] to [number] of "[exhibit]".
- … the letter dated [date] (a copy of which is now produced and shown to me marked "[exhibit]") …

Cover sheet and index (**HK**; for an affidavit read "sworn" for "affirmed"):

- This is the exhibit marked "[exhibit]" referred to in the [number] Affirmation of [deponent] affirmed on [date].
- These are the exhibits marked "[exhibit 1]" to "[exhibit 9]" referred to in the [number] Affirmation of [deponent] affirmed on [date].
- Before me, {signature} Commissioner for Oaths {or: Solicitor, Hong Kong SAR}
- Index columns: Exhibit | Date | Description | No. of pages

Witness statement (**HK**):

- A copy of the [document title] is at [Bundle ref].
- A copy of the [document title] dated [date] is annexed hereto marked "Annexure [number]".

US:

- Attached as Exhibit [exhibit] is a true and correct copy of the [document title] dated [date].

## 8. Reply

- **HK.** Save as expressly admitted below, I deny the allegations in [name]'s Affirmation.
- **HK, company.** Save as expressly admitted below, the [Plaintiff] denies the allegations in [name]'s Affirmation, and leaves matters of law to its counsel.
- **HK.** Where I do not respond to a particular paragraph of [name]'s Affirmation, that is not to be taken as an admission of its contents.
- **HK.** At paragraph [number] of [name]'s Affirmation, [name] says that "{a few words}". That is not correct.
- **HK.** I do not accept this explanation.
- **HK.** (which is denied)

## 9. Enforcement (HK)

Preamble:

- By a Judgment dated [date 1] (the "Judgment"), the Court entered judgment for the [Plaintiff] (the "Judgment Creditor") against the [Defendant] (the "Judgment Debtor") in the sum of [sum 1] (the "Judgment Sum"), together with interest and costs.
- By an Order dated [date 2] (the "Costs Order"), the Judgment Debtor was ordered to pay the Judgment Creditor's costs of {the action}, {summarily assessed / taxed} at [sum 2].
- As at [date 3], nothing has been paid under the Judgment or the Costs Order, and the following sums are due: (a) the Judgment Sum of [sum 1]; (b) interest on the Judgment Sum to [date 3] of [sum 3]; (c) the costs of [sum 2]; and (d) interest on those costs to [date 3] of [sum 4], a total of [sum 5] (the "Judgment Debt").
- The Judgment Debtor's full name is [name]. {His} last known address is [address].
- By a letter dated [date 4], sent on my instructions to the Judgment Debtor at [address] by {each mode of sending}, the Judgment Creditor demanded payment of [sum 6] by [date 5]. The Judgment Debtor has paid nothing.
- The Judgment Debt, with further interest to the date of payment, may now be enforced against the Judgment Debtor.

Charging order:

- To the best of my knowledge, information and belief, the Judgment Debtor has no creditors other than {each mortgagee or chargee, with the date of the instrument and its registration particulars}.
- ALL THAT piece or parcel of ground registered in the Land Registry as [property description] And of and in the messuages erections and buildings thereon known as [address] (the "Property").
- The land search shows the Judgment Debtor as the only registered owner of the Property. I verily believe that {he} alone is beneficially entitled to it, subject to {the registered encumbrances}.

Garnishee order:

- To the best of my knowledge, information and belief, [Garnishee] (the "Garnishee") is within the jurisdiction and is indebted to the Judgment Debtor in respect of monies standing to the credit of account no. [account number] in the name of the Judgment Debtor, maintained at its branch at [address] (branch code [number]).
- The grounds of my belief are {the matters set out above}.
- all debts due or accruing due from the Garnishee to the Judgment Debtor

Examination of the judgment debtor:

- … attend before {the Court or an officer of the Court} and be orally examined as to whether any and what debts are owing to {him}, and whether {he} has any and what other property or means of satisfying the Judgment Debt, and produce {the documents listed} …

Costs:

- … and that the costs of and occasioned by this application be paid by the Judgment Debtor
- … if the Court so directs, out of the monies attached

## 10. Prayer (HK)

- I therefore humbly pray for an order in terms of the Summons.
- In the circumstances, I respectfully ask the Court for an order in terms of the Summons.
- In the premises, I respectfully ask the Court to dismiss the [Defendant]'s Summons dated [date].
- If the Court is not minded to make {the first order}, I respectfully ask in the alternative for {the second order}.
- **HK Family.** A short closing courtesy in the deponent's own words is common: "I am grateful to the Court for its assistance."

## 11. Jurats and closing words (HK)

Affirmation (bracket layout; the deponent signs to the right):

```
AFFIRMED at the office of Messrs. [solicitors-firm],   )
[address], Hong Kong                                   )
this [date]                                            )    ......................
                                                       )    [deponent]
Before me,                                             )

......................
{Commissioner for Oaths / Solicitor, Hong Kong SAR}
```

- **Affidavit.** "SWORN at …" in place of "AFFIRMED at …".
- **Made outside Hong Kong.** "AFFIRMED at [address] this [date]. Before me, {signature} Notary Public". Check what the court requires for evidence made abroad (notarisation, authentication).
- **Filing line.** This Affirmation is filed on behalf of the [Plaintiff].
- **Interpreter's jurat** (where the deponent does not read English), after the deponent's jurat, with its own jurat: I, [name], of [address], do solemnly and sincerely affirm and say that I well understand [language] and the English language and that I have truly, distinctly and audibly interpreted the contents of this document to the Affirmant. {Change the language to the deponent's; older forms say "[language]".}
- **Witness statement, statement of truth.** I, [witness], believe that the facts stated in this Witness Statement are true. {Where the statement expresses an opinion, recorded practice adds: "and the opinion expressed in it is honestly held". Check the current form.}
- **Witness statement, interpreter's or translator's certificate.** The certificate in `references/jurisdictions/hk.md` ("Verification and evidence"), with "interpreted" for an oral reading and "translated" for a written one, and the right pronoun.

## 12. US closing words

- **Fed, signed inside the United States.** I declare under penalty of perjury that the foregoing is true and correct. Executed on [date]. {signature}
- **Fed, signed outside the United States.** I declare under penalty of perjury under the laws of the United States of America that the foregoing is true and correct. Executed on [date]. {signature}
- **NY (CPLR 2106).** I affirm this [date], under the penalties of perjury under the laws of New York, which may include a fine or imprisonment, that the foregoing is true, except as to matters alleged on information and belief and as to those matters I believe it to be true, and I understand that this document may be filed in an action or proceeding in a court of law. {signature} {The statute writes the date as "this ___ day of ______, ____".}
- **CA (CCP 2015.5), use everywhere.** I declare under penalty of perjury under the laws of the State of California that the foregoing is true and correct. Executed on [date] at [address]. {signature} {The in-state form omits "under the laws of the State of California" but then must state the date and place; the form above satisfies both.}
- **WA (RCW 5.50.050, meeting GR 13).** I declare under penalty of perjury under the law of Washington that the foregoing is true and correct. Signed on [date] at [address]. {printed name} {signature}
- **DE affidavit.** SWORN TO AND SUBSCRIBED before me this [date]. {signature} Notary Public. {The profiles show affidavits on personal knowledge under Superior Court Civil Rule 56(e) and Chancery Rule 56(c)(4); whether an unsworn declaration is accepted is for the user to check.}
- **NY word count.** Where Commercial Division Rule 17 or 22 NYCRR 202.8-b applies, counsel's certificate of the word count goes on a page attached at the end.

## 13. Connectives that are trade register

at all material times; In the premises; In the circumstances; Up to the date hereof; To date; For completeness; In any event; failed and/or refused; save as aforesaid; inter alia; the said.
