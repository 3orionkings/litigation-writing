# Advice formulae

These are the fixed forms of a counsel's advice. Use them word for word and keep them through the register pass. The jurisdiction tag says where each belongs: **HK** (Hong Kong counsel's advice), **US** (a US privileged memorandum or opinion letter), **both**. Ellipses mark counsel's own words; bracketed labels are fields.

## Heading and privilege

| Formula | Where |
|---|---|
| "PRIVILEGED AND CONFIDENTIAL" | both |
| "ATTORNEY-CLIENT COMMUNICATION" / "ATTORNEY WORK PRODUCT" | US |
| "ADVICE ON …" / "PRELIMINARY ADVICE ON …" | HK |
| "TO: … / FROM: … / DATE: [date] / RE: …" | US |
| "Re: … (Point Form for Oral Advice)" | both, point form |

## Instruction and basis

| Formula | Where |
|---|---|
| "Instructing solicitors, [solicitors-firm], act for [Party] in the above action." | HK |
| "I am instructed by a letter dated [date] ("the Instructions") to advise on …" | HK |
| "I am asked to advise on … . In particular, I am asked to:" | both |
| "I am asked to consider whether …" | both |
| "I am also instructed to address the following:" | HK |
| "… on the documents provided to me and listed in Annex 1." | both |
| "I am instructed that …" (a fact that comes only from the instructions) | both |
| "… is and was at all material times …" / "at the material time" | both |

## Opinion and hedge

| Formula | Where |
|---|---|
| "I am of the view that" / "I am of the preliminary view that" / "I am not of the view that" | both |
| "I incline to take the view that" / "I incline to take the preliminary view that" | HK |
| "I recommend that [Party] consider …" | US |
| "on the information provided" / "on the basis of the materials provided and at this stage" | both |
| "subject to further information" / "subject to further instructions" | both |
| "subject to [Party] coming up to proof" | HK |
| "on the balance of probabilities" | HK |
| "by a preponderance of the evidence" | US |
| "taking [Party]'s case at its highest" | both |
| "Even if I am mistaken in that, …" | both |
| "I am in no position to assess … on the information provided." | both |
| "It is premature to express a view on …" | both |

## The verbal likelihood scale

Weakest to strongest for the party advised. Never a number.

| Grade | Words |
|---|---|
| Hopeless | "bound to fail"; "cannot get off the ground"; "would hold no water" |
| Weak | "unlikely to succeed"; "very difficult to prove"; "highly unlikely" |
| Open | "arguable"; "at least arguable"; "a live issue for argument" |
| Balanced | "finely balanced" |
| Better than even | "slightly more likely than not"; "more likely than not" |
| Good | "likely to succeed"; "inherently more probable" |

## Pivots and signposts

| Formula | Where |
|---|---|
| "Applying the principles here, the question to ask is as follows:" | both |
| "Applying the legal test here, for the first limb … . For the second limb …" | both |
| "The principles can be distilled as follows. First, … . Second, … . Finally, … ." | both |
| "The test is threefold. First, … ("Step 1"). Second, … ("Step 2"). Third, … ("Step 3")." | both |
| "Scenario 1: … . Scenario 2: …" | both |
| "Is it a defence that …?" then "This would not assist [Party], because …" | both |
| "Pausing here, …" / "Turning back to …" / "In the present case, …" | both |
| "Put simply, …" / "In other words, …" / "For completeness, …" | both |
| "It follows that …" / "In light of the above, …" / "In the circumstances, …" | both |
| "In interim conclusion, I incline to take the view that …" | HK |
| "Interim Conclusion" (as a heading) | both |

## Law and quotation

| Formula | Where |
|---|---|
| "The applicable principles are set out in … at …:" followed by the indented quotation | both |
| "the learned authors of …, … edition, at §…" (a textbook only if the user supplied it) | HK |
| "per [judge] at [§ needed]" | HK |
| "(emphasis added)" after every quotation with added emphasis | both |
| "(sic)" after an error in the original | both |
| "…" for an omission inside a quotation | both |

## Missing information

| Formula | Where |
|---|---|
| "I have no information regarding …" / "I do not have instructions regarding …" | both |
| "No documents have been provided on …, and I am unable to comment further on it." | both |
| "A reasoned assessment needs further information on …" | both |
| "(see item (c) of paragraph [number] above)" | both |
| "I would be grateful if instructing solicitors would provide the following information:" | HK |
| "Any of it may change the views expressed in this advice." | both |

## Pleading hand-off

| Formula | Where |
|---|---|
| "… can, and should, be pleaded." | both |
| "There is presently insufficient material to plead …" | both |
| "Particulars to plead. The pleading should include the following:" | HK |
| "Particulars to plead (facts to allege). The complaint should allege:" | US |

| "Further and/or other relief as the Court deems fit." / "Costs." | HK |

## Close

| Formula | Where |
|---|---|
| "Taking the issues together, I incline to take the preliminary view that …" | HK |
| "My views are subject to the information provided, and may change as further information is obtained and the witnesses come up to proof." | both |
| "They make no allowance for the hazards of litigation." | both |
| "[Party] may wish to consider mediation." | both |
| "The foregoing is my preliminary advice on these matters." | both |
| "I would be happy to assist or advise further if so required." | both |
| "If I can be of any further assistance, please do not hesitate to contact me." | both |

| "[counsel-name]" then "[solicitors-firm]" | US |
