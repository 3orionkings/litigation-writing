# List of authorities

## What it is for

The list that accompanies a skeleton, opening, appeal or costs paper and tells the court and the other side which authorities will be relied on, so that a bundle can be prepared. It is mechanical, and mistakes in it are noticed: a tag that points to the wrong case, a case cited in the text but missing from the list, a list entry never cited.

**Only the user's authorities.** Every entry comes from the matter file's `authorities` list or from the user in the conversation (see `references/authorities.md`). Never add an authority to make the list look complete.

## Structure: Hong Kong

**Caption.** Repeat the caption of the paper it accompanies exactly: hearing line, action number, and the paper's title, then the list's own title (`LIST OF AUTHORITIES FOR THE [Plaintiff]`; `SUPPLEMENTAL LIST OF AUTHORITIES FOR THE [Plaintiff]`; or an ordinal in a sequence).

**Body.**

1. **Groups.** Legislation and rules first, then cases, then textbooks and other materials, each under its own heading. Practice directions go with the rules.
2. **Order.** Within each group, the order of first citation in the paper. Number continuously across the groups, so the list number is the tag number used in the text (`[P#3]` is item 3).
3. **Entries.** One line each. A case: full name, neutral citation and report citation where both exist; for an unreported case without a neutral citation, the action number and date ("(unreported, [date])"). **No pinpoint**: the pinpoint belongs in the paper, at the point of reliance. Legislation: the title, the chapter or code, and the sections or rules relied on. A textbook: title, edition, year, volume, and the paragraph range of the extract supplied.
4. **Sequences.** A supplemental list continues the numbering of the last list. Do not reuse a number.
5. **The other side's authorities.** Do not re-list an authority already in the other side's list; cite it in the paper by their tag (`[D#4]`). If the court supplied an authority, list it under its own tag scheme (`[Court#1]`) or as the user directs.
6. **Signature.** Signed and dated with the same block as the paper.

**Cross-check before returning.** Every tag in the paper appears in the list with the same number; every list entry is cited in the paper; no two entries share a tag; each entry's citation matches the citation in the text.

## Differences in the US profiles

US practice puts a **table of authorities** inside the brief instead of, or as well as, a separate list: cases (alphabetical), then constitutional provisions, statutes, rules and other authorities, each with the page numbers of the brief where it is cited ("passim" where it appears throughout). Courts require it above a set length (for example N.D. Cal. Civil L.R. 7-4(a)(2); CRC 3.1113(f); D. Del. LR 7.1.3, "table of citations"; Ct. Ch. R. 7(c)(2)(B)). Citation form follows the profile (Bluebook; New York Official Reports; California Style Manual; Washington style). Some courts or judges also require copies of unreported decisions to be supplied; check the profile and the judge's practices. The table is alphabetical, not in order of first citation, and carries no tags.

## Skeleton (Hong Kong)

```
For hearing before [judge] on [date 1] at [time]
[case number]

SKELETON SUBMISSIONS OF THE [Plaintiff]
LIST OF AUTHORITIES FOR THE [Plaintiff]

A. Legislation and Rules

1. [rule]

2. [statutory provision]

B. Cases

3. [authority needed: the case supplied by the user for the first proposition]

4. [authority needed: the case supplied by the user for the second proposition]

C. Textbooks and Other Materials

5. [document title], [number] edition, paragraphs … to …

[counsel-name]
Counsel for the [Plaintiff]
[date 2]
```

## Checklist

The checker tests: a numbered list; no pinpoints in the entries; legislation in its own group (soft); a signature (soft). It does not number paragraphs as prose.

Check by eye:

- Numbers match the tags used in the paper, in order of first citation.
- Every authority cited in the paper is listed, and every listed authority is cited.
- No entry was added that the user did not supply.
- The caption matches the paper it accompanies.
