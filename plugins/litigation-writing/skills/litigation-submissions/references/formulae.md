# Formulae for submissions

These are the fixed forms of the advocate's trade. Keep them word for word: do not vary them for style, and do not let the register pass rewrite them. Fill the brackets from the matter file or leave the placeholder. Where a formula belongs to one system, the group says so. Everything else is common to Hong Kong and the US profiles, adjusted to US spelling ("defense", "favor") in a US paper.

Use them where they fit, not as decoration. A paper that opens three paragraphs running with a signpost reads as padded.

## 1. Caption, legend and title (Hong Kong)

- `For hearing before [judge] on [date] at [time]`
- `For trial before [judge] on [date] at [time]`
- `For the hearing on [date] at [time]` (appeal court papers, which normally do not name the bench)
- `SKELETON SUBMISSIONS OF THE [Plaintiff]`
- `[Plaintiff]'S HEARING NOTE`
- `OPENING SUBMISSIONS OF THE [Defendant]` / `CLOSING SUBMISSIONS OF THE [Plaintiff]`
- `[Plaintiff]'S SUBMISSIONS ON COSTS PURSUANT TO THE DIRECTION OF [judge] DATED [date]`
- `[number] SKELETON SUBMISSIONS OF THE [Appellant] (IN REPLY TO THE [Respondent]'S SKELETON SUBMISSIONS DATED [date])`
- `(to be read with the [Plaintiff]'s Skeleton Submissions dated [date])` (a supplemental paper)
- `[A12/§3] = Bundle A, page 12, paragraph 3` (the legend line; use the user's own scheme)
- `[D§n] = paragraph n of the [Defendant]'s Skeleton Submissions dated [date]`
- `[Decision/§n] = paragraph n of the judgment of [judge] dated [date]`
- `Abbreviations: the [Plaintiff] ("P"); the [Defendant] ("D").`

## 2. Opening (Hong Kong; usable anywhere)

- `This is the hearing of the [Defendant]'s Summons dated [date] ("the Summons") seeking that:`
- `This is the hearing of the following [number] applications:`
- `This is the [number] call-over hearing of …` / `This matter was last adjourned on [date] by consent.`
- `These submissions are filed under the direction of [judge] dated [date].`
- `These are the [Plaintiff]'s submissions in reply to the [Defendant]'s Skeleton Submissions dated [date].`
- `This is the trial of the [Plaintiff]'s claim for …, fixed for [number] days by order of [judge] dated [date] [Bundle ref].`
- `The [Applicant] applies for an extension of time and for leave to appeal from the decision of [judge] dated [date] [Bundle ref].`
- `In gist, the [Plaintiff]'s stance is that:`
- `It is common ground that …` / `It is not in dispute that:`
- `The orders sought are set out at paragraph [number] below.`

## 3. Signposts and argument forms (all systems)

- `It is submitted that` / `It is respectfully submitted that`
- `In the premises,`
- `In any event,`
- `Further and/or alternatively,`
- `taken at its highest (which is denied)`
- `Even if …, which is denied, …`
- `(which is not admitted)`
- `If the answer to Step 1 is yes (which is denied), …`
- `A fortiori`
- `Suffice to say`
- `It is trite that`
- `For completeness,`
- `Insofar as … is concerned,`
- `Applying those principles:`
- `The [Defendant]'s application fails on this ground alone.`
- `(emphasis added)` / `(sic)` / `(as he then was)` / `(as she then was)`
- `the learned editors of …` (a practice textbook, with edition, paragraph and authority tag)

## 4. Answering the other side (all systems)

- `[D§12-14]:` as the lead-in to a paragraph that answers those paragraphs
- `At [D§12], the [Defendant] contends that …. That is misconceived.`
- `For [D§20-25], the [Plaintiff] relies on [P1§30-34] and does not repeat that argument here.`
- Verdict words, used once a point and tied to a reference: `is misconceived`, `is no answer`, `does not assist the [Defendant]`, `is bound to fail`, `is devoid of merit`, `does not arise`.

## 5. Orders, directions and costs (Hong Kong)

- `The [Plaintiff] respectfully seeks the following orders:`
- `an order in terms of the Summons`
- `The Summons be dismissed with costs to the [Plaintiff].`
- `The [Defendant] do file and serve … within [period] days of the date of this Order.`
- `Unless the [Defendant] files and serves … by 4:00 pm on [date], …`
- `judgment be entered for the [Plaintiff] against the [Defendant] in the sum of [sum]`
- `together with interest on [sum] at [rate] per annum from [date] to the date of judgment and thereafter at judgment rate until payment`
- `The Summons be adjourned to a date to be fixed, with [period] reserved.`
- `Liberty to apply.`
- `Costs in the cause.` / `Costs reserved.` / `No order as to costs.` / `Costs here and below.`
- `costs of and occasioned by the Summons be paid by the [Defendant] to the [Plaintiff]`
- `on a party and party basis` / `on an indemnity basis`
- `with a certificate for counsel` / `with a certificate for two counsel`
- `to be taxed if not agreed`
- `summarily assessed in the sum of [sum] and paid within [period] days` / `and paid forthwith`
- `including all reserved costs` / `costs thrown away`
- `The [Plaintiff] is grateful for the Court's assistance.` (hearing notes only)

## 6. Appeals (Hong Kong; adapt elsewhere)

- `The Judge erred in law in holding that … [Decision/§n].`
- `It follows that the Judge erred in …`
- `The time for filing and serving the notice of appeal be extended to [date].`
- `Leave to appeal be granted on Grounds [number] and [number].`
- `The appeal be allowed, the order of [judge] dated [date] be set aside, and in its place …`

## 7. Trials (all systems)

- `From the pleadings, the issues for determination are:`
- `The Court is invited to find that:`
- `If the answer to Issue 1 is no, Issues 2 and 3 do not arise.`
- `The Court is invited to give little weight to the evidence of [witness] on this point.`
- `The Court is invited to draw an adverse inference from the [Defendant]'s failure to produce …`
- `Costs follow the event.`

## 8. Costs submissions (Hong Kong)

- `The [Plaintiff] does not dispute that the costs of … should be paid by the [Plaintiff] to the [Defendant] on a party and party basis.`
- `The [Plaintiff] resists each of the uplifts sought:`
- `A certificate for counsel is neither necessary nor proper.`
- `to mark the Court's disapproval of the [Defendant]'s conduct of these proceedings, namely:`

## 9. United States

Notice of motion, New York Supreme Court:

> `PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that, upon the affirmation of [name] dated [date], the exhibits annexed to it, and the accompanying memorandum of law, [Defendant] will move this Court at the Motion Submission Part, Room [number], of the courthouse at [address], on [date] at [time], or as soon thereafter as counsel can be heard, for an order …`

Notice of motion, federal court and California:

> `PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that on [date] at [time], or as soon thereafter as the matter may be heard, in Courtroom [number] of the above-entitled Court, located at [address], [Defendant] will, and hereby does, move the Court for an order …`

Headings by profile (rule-prescribed where a rule is named):

- Federal and New York: `PRELIMINARY STATEMENT` / `STATEMENT OF FACTS` / `ARGUMENT` (`POINT I`, `POINT II` in New York) / `CONCLUSION`
- California: `NOTICE OF MOTION AND MOTION` / `MEMORANDUM OF POINTS AND AUTHORITIES`
- King County (KC LCR 7(b)(5)(B)): `RELIEF REQUESTED` / `STATEMENT OF FACTS` / `STATEMENT OF ISSUES` / `EVIDENCE RELIED UPON` / `ARGUMENT`
- District of Delaware (LR 7.1.3(b)): `NATURE AND STAGE OF THE PROCEEDINGS` / `SUMMARY OF ARGUMENT` / `STATEMENT OF FACTS` / `ARGUMENT` / `CONCLUSION`
- Washington, first page: `ORAL ARGUMENT REQUESTED` (KC LCR 7(b)(4)(C))

Close and signature:

- `For the foregoing reasons, [Defendant] respectfully requests that the Court grant the motion and enter the proposed order submitted with it.`
- `Respectfully submitted,` / `Dated: [date]` / `[counsel-name] ([bar number])` / `Attorneys for [Defendant]`

Word-count certificates:

- Washington (KC LCR 7(b)(5)(B)(vi) and LCR 56(c)(3); W.D. Wash. LCR 7(e)(6)): `I certify that this memorandum contains [number] words, in compliance with the Local Civil Rules.`
- New York (22 NYCRR 202.8-b(c); Commercial Division Rule 17), on a separate last page: `I certify that this memorandum of law contains [number] words, excluding the parts exempted by 22 NYCRR 202.8-b(b), and complies with the applicable word limit.`
- Delaware Chancery (Ct. Ch. R. 10(d)), in the signature block: `Word limit: [number]. Words in this brief subject to the limit: [number].`
- Delaware Superior (Super. Ct. Civ. R. 107(h)(2)): `This brief complies with the type-volume limitation of Superior Court Civil Rule 107(h)(1) because it contains [number] words.`

Record citations (use the court's form, never a Hong Kong bundle tag):

- `ECF No. [number] at [number]` (federal); `D.I. [number]` (District of Delaware); `Dkt. # [number] at p. [number]` (W.D. Wash.); `NYSCEF Doc. No. [number]` (New York)
- `[witness] Decl. ¶ [number]; Ex. [exhibit] at [number]` (California requires exhibit, page and line, CRC 3.1113(k))

California separate statement columns (CRC 3.1350(h)):

- `Moving Party's Undisputed Material Facts and Supporting Evidence:` / `Opposing Party's Response and Supporting Evidence:` / `Undisputed.` / `Disputed.`

## 10. Speaking notes (private, first person allowed)

- `Primary position:` / `Fallback, only if the Court is against me on the primary position:`
- `If they say: … → Reply: … [Bundle ref]`
- `Concede no more than: …`
- `Order of argument:`

## 11. Lists of authorities and chronologies

- `LIST OF AUTHORITIES FOR THE [Plaintiff]` / `SUPPLEMENTAL LIST OF AUTHORITIES FOR THE [Plaintiff]`
- Group headings: `A. Legislation and Rules` / `B. Cases` / `C. Textbooks and Other Materials`
- `(unreported, [date])`
- `AGREED CHRONOLOGY` / `APPENDIX [number]: THE [Defendant]'S CHRONOLOGY`
- `[Plaintiff] = P; [Defendant] = D`
- `returnable on [date], with [period] reserved`
